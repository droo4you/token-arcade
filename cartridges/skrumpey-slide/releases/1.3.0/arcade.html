How to Play

Slide, fly, grab DN and bring your buddies home before dark.

The goal

Get the biggest score before nightfall: go far, fly high, land clean, bring friends.

How a run ends

  • Only nightfall ends a run. The sun bar at the top starts with 60 seconds of daylight.
  • Every perfect landing adds +0.5 s of daylight. In a lane sprint the sun sets at 3/4 speed.
  • Falling into a gap doesn't end it. You pop back out just past the gap at a reset speed of about 20. You lose any power-up and your combo goes back to x1.
  • Getting hit (critters, spikes, sprint barriers) doesn't end it either.
    • With a train: your last follower hops off and you keep 90% speed.
    • No train: you keep only 60% speed, lose your power-up and your combo resets.
    • Either way you blink for about 1.2 s and can't be hit again.
  • Bad landings (nose-diving more than ~49° into the slope) keep 80% speed and reset your combo.

Controls

Desktop

  • Hold mouse / S / ↓ / Space = dive: gravity gets way stronger and you speed up on downhills. Let go before an uphill to launch!
  • ↑/W = jump from the ground (uses a charge)
  • ←→ / AD = switch lanes in sprints
  • N = music on/off

Mobile

  • Hold DIVE or the playfield to dive; release to fly
  • JUMP (bottom right) = jump. In runner sections, swipe the playfield to change lanes; tap to hop. DIVE is hidden during these sections.
  • Swipe left/right = switch lanes in sprints
  • button = music

Slingshot: hold to pull back. The power meter swings up and down (one full swing every 1.5 s), so let go near the top. 90%+ is a PERFECT launch and gives +8 extra launch speed. If you don't pull, it auto-fires at 30% after 10 s.

Jump charges: you start with 3 and can hold up to 5. You get +1 for each new biome (every 640 m) and +1 every time your combo hits a multiple of 5 on a perfect landing. You can only jump from the ground.

How points work

Score = distance in meters + all your bonus points. Most bonuses get multiplied by your train multiplier (TM), and some by your combo too.

Power-ups

  • Star (6 s): a +25% speed burst, minimum speed 18, smash critters for 40 × TM, no bad landings, and nothing can hit you. With a Star-trait buddy in your train it lasts 9 s (×1.5).
  • Beam (9 s): auto-zaps the nearest critter ahead (within 34 m) every 0.4 s. With a Beam-trait buddy in your train it also shoots backwards (up to 22 m behind).
  • Trait buddies: 4 Skrumpeys carry Star, 3 carry Beam, the other 4 are just good company.

Skrumpey Train

  • Stranded Skrumpeys wait on hilltops, ramps, rest spots and sprint lanes. Slide or fly within ~4 m to rescue them.
  • Each follower adds +0.2 to your train multiplier, up to ×2.5 at 8 followers. Extra followers still show up on your squad card.
  • Get hit with a train and your last follower hops off instead of you losing speed and power. Basically extra lives.

Lane sprints & DN frenzy

  • Between hill sections the camera swings behind you for a 320 m, 2-stage lane sprint (Hedge Run, Dune Canyon, Crystal Tunnel, Cloud Bridge). Stage speeds rise from 36 to 42.
  • Lane sprints run in 2 stages, each faster and busier: zigzags, corridors, slaloms, full-width hurdles (hop them) and splits with a rare nut on one side. Switch lanes with ←/→ or a swipe. Press W / ↑ (or tap on touchscreens) for a free hop (no charge used) over barriers. Hitting a barrier counts as a hit. Nuts and buddies sit in the lanes too. Every sprint is followed by rolling hills and a big ramp before another biome transition.
  • Every nut fills the nut meter (+1, rare +3). At 45 it's DN FRENZY: a small speed kick helps you recover momentum, then for 5 s nuts rain down (one every 1/6 s, 30 total) and a big magnet (9 m) pulls in nearby nuts.

Quick tips

  • Dive down, let go up. That's the rhythm.
  • Chain perfect landings: the combo boosts flights, nuts and stomps, and each one buys daylight.
  • Get to 8 followers fast. ×2.5 on every bonus adds up fast.
  • Your train is your shield.
  • Save jump charges to clear a nasty spike or rescue a buddy up on a ledge.
