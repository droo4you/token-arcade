# Skrumpey starter assets

Source: public SKRUMP collection (Monad, contract 0xB0DAD798C80e40Dd6b8E8545074C6a5B7B97D2c0, 3,333 tokens, by melo).
- PNG: on-chain tokenURI image via evmfs.xyz (640x640). Metadata: tokenURI via Monad RPC -> evmfs.xyz.
- 3D: per-trait GLBs from https://www.skrumpeys.net/models/<slot>/<value>-optimized.glb (Draco-compressed originals in models/parts/). models/<id>.glb is all of the token's parts merged into one scene (Draco decoded, uncompressed) by a script; parts share a common origin as on the site viewer, but alignment was not visually verified.
- Not included: the site's `cheeky` slot (derived, not in tokenURI metadata) and non-3D traits (background, aura, scene, extra). Ownership/licensing of the art not confirmed; check with the creator before shipping commercially.
